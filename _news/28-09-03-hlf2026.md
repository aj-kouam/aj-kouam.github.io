Delighted to be selected to return to the [Heidelberg Laureate Forum 2026](https://www.heidelberg-laureate-forum.org/forum/13th-hlf-2026/program-13th-hlf-2026/) as an alumna! 
I had the opportunity to facilitate and contribute to discussions with recipients of some of the world's most prestigious scientific distinctions (i.e., the ACM A.M. Turing Award, Fields Medal, Abel Prize, and Nobel Prize) as well as young researchers from around the world, on research, academic life, and the role of mathematics and computer science in society. Some memories from the week are captured in the HLF [photo album](https://www.flickr.com/photos/hlforum/albums/72177720335631079/).
